1. Ambitious Healthy Hot Chocolate Mugs

1. Ambitious Healthy Hot Chocolate Mugs

⏱️ 20 Mins (5m prep, 15m heat) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$1.80/serving) 🍽️ Serves 4
Save Pin
A silky, antioxidant‑rich hot chocolate that feels indulgent without the guilt.

This guilt‑free hot chocolate balances deep cocoa richness with a subtle hint of natural sweetness from maple syrup, while a splash of almond milk adds a nutty whisper. The velvety texture is crowned with airy coconut whipped cream and a dusting of cinnamon, releasing a warm, comforting aroma that instantly feels like a holiday hug. Guests love its creamy mouthfeel paired with a light, health‑forward twist.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 tbsp raw cacao powder
  • 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch of sea salt
  • 1/4 cup coconut cream (chilled)
  • ground cinnamon for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a medium saucepan over medium heat, whisk almond milk, raw cacao powder, maple syrup, vanilla extract, and sea salt together until the cocoa fully dissolves and the mixture is smooth
  2. Step 2: bring to a gentle simmer, not a boil, for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent scorching
  3. Step 3: while the chocolate heats, place a chilled mixing bowl and whisk attachment in the freezer for 10 minutes, then pour chilled coconut cream into the bowl and whip on high speed until soft peaks form, about 2–3 minutes
  4. Step 4: once the hot chocolate reaches a simmer, remove from heat and pour evenly into four mugs, leaving room for the whipped topping
  5. Step 5: dollop a generous spoonful of coconut whipped cream on each mug, then sprinkle with ground cinnamon and drizzle a thin line of extra maple syrup for extra shine
💡 Chef's Tip: For an extra boost of antioxidants, stir in a pinch of ground turmeric and a dash of black pepper just before serving.

Serve immediately while steaming, paired with a side of spiced ginger shortbread for a sweet‑savory combo; leftovers can be reheated gently over low heat, adding a splash more almond milk if needed.

2. Ambitious Pumpkin Spice Latte with Whipped Cream

⏱️ 15 Mins (5m prep, 10m steam) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 2
Save Pin
A cozy latte that marries pumpkin’s earthy sweetness with espresso’s bold bite.

This pumpkin spice latte delivers a harmonious blend of earthy pumpkin puree, aromatic cinnamon, nutmeg, and clove, balanced by the robust depth of espresso. The velvety steamed milk creates a silky mouthfeel, while the cloud‑light whipped cream adds a luscious finish that melts into the drink, releasing a fragrant, autumnal perfume. It’s the ultimate festive flavor that keeps guests reaching for another sip.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup milk (dairy or plant‑based)
  • 1/4 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tsp espresso or strong brewed coffee
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp powdered sugar for topping

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine milk, pumpkin puree, pumpkin pie spice, and brown sugar
  2. Step 2: whisk continuously until the mixture is smooth and begins to steam, about 4 minutes, being careful not to boil
  3. Step 3: meanwhile, brew a strong espresso shot or 2 tbsp of very strong coffee and set aside
  4. Step 4: once the pumpkin milk mixture is hot, use a handheld frother or whisk to create a light foam, then pour the espresso into two mugs, topping each with the steamed pumpkin milk
  5. Step 5: in a chilled bowl, whip heavy cream with powdered sugar until soft peaks form, then dollop a generous spoonful onto each latte
  6. Step 6: finish with an extra pinch of pumpkin pie spice for visual flair
💡 Chef's Tip: Freeze the pumpkin puree in ice‑cube trays ahead of time; this makes it easy to portion and prevents clumping when heating.

Serve warm alongside cinnamon‑sugar biscotti for a perfect dunk; any leftover latte can be chilled and turned into a pumpkin spice iced coffee the next day.

3. Averie Sparkling Christmas Punch (Pitcher)

⏱️ 10 Mins (5m prep, 5m chill) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$1.20/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8
Save Pin
A festive, effervescent punch that bubbles with cranberry‑orange zest and herbal aroma.

This sparkling Christmas punch bursts with tart cranberry juice, sweet orange zest, and a subtle herbal note from rosemary, all lifted by crisp club soda. The combination creates a refreshing, lightly sweet flavor profile that dances on the palate, while the aromatic rosemary adds an unexpected depth that makes the drink feel both celebratory and sophisticated. Perfect for holiday tables where guests crave a non‑alcoholic sparkle.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cranberry juice
  • 1 cup freshly squeezed orange juice
  • 1/2 cup simple syrup
  • 2 cups club soda
  • 1 orange, thinly sliced
  • 4 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • ice cubes

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large pitcher, combine cranberry juice, orange juice, and simple syrup
  2. Step 2: stir vigorously until the syrup is fully incorporated and the mixture takes on a deep ruby hue
  3. Step 3: add a handful of ice cubes to chill the base, then slowly pour club soda over the ice, allowing it to fizz and mix gently without losing carbonation
  4. Step 4: arrange orange slices around the inside of the pitcher for visual appeal and to infuse a bright citrus aroma
  5. Step 5: tuck rosemary sprigs in between the orange slices, pressing lightly so the fragrant oils are released
  6. Step 6: give the punch one final gentle stir to distribute the aromatics evenly before serving
💡 Chef's Tip: Prepare the simple syrup a day ahead and store it in the refrigerator; this saves time and ensures the syrup is perfectly clear.

Serve in clear glassware with a single rosemary sprig for garnish; any leftover punch can be refrigerated for up to 24 hours, adding a splash more club soda before serving to refresh the fizz.

4. Chelsea Raspberry Sherbet Punch (Bowl)

⏱️ 12 Mins (5m prep, 7m chill) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$1.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 10
Save Pin
A chilled, fruity punch that blends tangy raspberry sherbet with sparkling soda for a frosty delight.

This raspberry sherbet punch delivers a bright, tangy raspberry flavor balanced by the creamy coolness of sherbet and the lively sparkle of lemon‑lime soda. The texture is luxuriously slushy, offering a refreshing bite that melts slowly on the tongue, while fresh berries and mint add bursts of natural sweetness and a fragrant, cooling finish. It’s a crowd‑pleasing, eye‑catching drink that feels both nostalgic and modern.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 pint raspberry sherbet
  • 2 cups lemon‑lime soda (e.g., Sprite)
  • 1 cup cranberry juice
  • 1 cup fresh raspberries
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves
  • ice cubes

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large glass punch bowl, gently soften the raspberry sherbet by stirring it with a wooden spoon for about 2 minutes, creating a smooth, pourable base
  2. Step 2: slowly add lemon‑lime soda and cranberry juice, swirling the bowl to blend the sherbet into a frothy, pink liquid without over‑mixing
  3. Step 3: drop in a generous handful of fresh raspberries and a handful of mint leaves, allowing them to float and release their aroma
  4. Step 4: add a mound of ice cubes around the edges to keep the punch chilled
  5. Step 5: give the mixture one final gentle stir, ensuring the berries are evenly distributed and the mint leaves are lightly bruised for maximum flavor
💡 Chef's Tip: Freeze fresh raspberries on a baking sheet beforehand; they stay firm longer in the punch and add a decorative pop.

Ladle into chilled glasses with a mint sprig and a raspberry on the rim; leftovers can be frozen into icy pops for a festive dessert treat.

5. Chelsea Spiced Apple Cider Mug

⏱️ 15 Mins (5m prep, 10m simmer) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$1.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6
Save Pin
Aromatic spiced apple cider that fills the room with sweet, comforting holiday notes.

This classic spiced apple cider combines the natural sweetness of fresh apples with warm spices—cinnamon, cloves, and star anise—creating a fragrant, sweet‑spicy profile that feels like a cozy blanket on a chilly November evening. The liquid is silky and slightly thickened by the apple pulp, while the aromatic spices linger on the palate, delivering a comforting finish that pairs beautifully with savory bites and sweet treats alike.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 8 cups apple juice or fresh pressed cider
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 2 star anise pods
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 orange, thinly sliced
  • optional whipped cream for topping

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large saucepan over medium heat, combine apple juice, cinnamon sticks, cloves, star anise, and orange slices
  2. Step 2: bring to a gentle simmer, stirring occasionally, and let the spices infuse for 8 minutes, watching for a fragrant steam rise
  3. Step 3: reduce heat to low and stir in maple syrup, allowing it to dissolve fully
  4. Step 4: continue to simmer for an additional 2 minutes, ensuring the cider stays hot but does not boil over
  5. Step 5: remove the saucepan from heat and strain the cider through a fine‑mesh sieve into a heat‑proof pitcher, discarding the whole spices and orange slices
  6. Step 6: ladle the hot cider into six rustic mugs, topping each with a dollop of whipped cream if desired and a fresh cinnamon stick for garnish
💡 Chef's Tip: Prepare the spiced cider a day ahead and gently reheat on low; the flavors deepen and meld beautifully overnight.

Serve immediately with a side of caramel‑drizzled pecan bites; any leftover cider can be stored refrigerated for up to 3 days and reheated gently on the stove.

2. DamnDelicious Cranberry Punch (Pitcher)

6. DamnDelicious Cranberry Punch (Pitcher)

⏱️ 12 Mins (5m prep, 7m chill) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$1.30/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8
Save Pin
A bold, tangy cranberry punch that balances sweet and tart with a hint of herbaceous rosemary.

This cranberry punch offers a vibrant, tart cranberry base sweetened just enough with agave nectar, while a splash of lime juice adds bright acidity. Fresh rosemary introduces an earthy, piney aroma that elevates the drink from ordinary to unforgettable, and the effervescent sparkle of ginger ale adds a lively finish. The result is a balanced, festive flavor that pairs perfectly with both sweet desserts and savory turkey dishes.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cranberry juice
  • 1 cup ginger ale
  • 1/4 cup agave nectar
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • lime wedges for garnish
  • ice cubes

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large pitcher, combine cranberry juice, agave nectar, and freshly squeezed lime juice
  2. Step 2: whisk until the agave is fully dissolved and the mixture takes on a glossy ruby hue
  3. Step 3: add a handful of ice cubes to chill the base, then slowly pour ginger ale over the ice, allowing it to fizz and integrate without losing carbonation
  4. Step 4: gently stir in two rosemary sprigs, pressing them lightly so the fragrant oils are released
  5. Step 5: arrange lime wedges around the rim of the pitcher for visual pop and an extra burst of citrus when squeezed
  6. Step 6: give the punch a final gentle swirl to distribute the aromatics evenly before serving
💡 Chef's Tip: Lightly bruise rosemary sprigs with the back of a spoon before adding to release more flavor without overpowering the punch.

Serve in clear glasses with a rosemary sprig garnish; leftovers keep well refrigerated for 24 hours, topped with a splash of fresh ginger ale before serving to revive the fizz.

7. DinnerAtTheZoo Lime Sherbet Punch (Bowl)

⏱️ 10 Mins (5m prep, 5m chill) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$1.40/serving) 🍽️ Serves 10
Save Pin
A zesty, frosty punch that fuses tangy lime sherbet with crisp cucumber for ultimate refreshment.

This lime sherbet punch delivers an electrifying burst of tangy lime paired with the cool, clean taste of cucumber, creating a refreshing, palate‑cleansing experience. The sherbet’s icy texture melts slowly, providing a creamy mouthfeel, while the cucumber adds subtle vegetal crunch and a hint of freshness. A splash of sparkling water lifts the flavor, making it a lively, festive beverage that’s perfect for warm Thanksgiving evenings.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 pint lime sherbet
  • 2 cups sparkling water
  • 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup fresh lime juice
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • fresh mint leaves for garnish
  • ice cubes

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large glass punch bowl, allow the lime sherbet to soften slightly by stirring with a spatula for about 2 minutes, achieving a pourable consistency
  2. Step 2: pour in sparkling water and freshly squeezed lime juice, whisking gently to combine the sherbet into a frothy, bright green liquid
  3. Step 3: drizzle honey over the mixture and stir until fully incorporated, ensuring the honey dissolves completely
  4. Step 4: add cucumber slices and a handful of ice cubes, allowing the cucumber to infuse its cool flavor into the punch
  5. Step 5: finish with a generous scattering of fresh mint leaves, giving the bowl a final gentle stir to distribute the aromatics
💡 Chef's Tip: Freeze cucumber slices on a tray before adding; they stay crisp longer and keep the punch colder without diluting flavor.

Serve in chilled glasses with a cucumber ribbon garnish; any leftover punch can be frozen into lime sherbet pops for a fun after‑dinner treat.

8. EatingOnADime Christmas Punch (Pitcher)

⏱️ 15 Mins (5m prep, 10m chill) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$1.10/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8
Save Pin
A rich, ruby‑red punch that blends cranberry, pomegranate, and citrus for holiday sparkle.

This Christmas punch blends the deep tartness of cranberry with the jewel‑like burst of pomegranate, while orange zest adds a bright citrus note that lifts the whole profile. A touch of vanilla bean syrup rounds out the flavors with subtle sweetness, creating a balanced, festive drink that feels both sophisticated and comforting. The effervescent splash of club soda adds a lively fizz, making each sip sparkle like holiday lights.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cranberry juice
  • 1 cup pomegranate juice
  • 1/2 cup vanilla bean simple syrup
  • juice of 1 orange
  • 2 cups club soda
  • 1/4 cup pomegranate seeds
  • orange zest for garnish
  • ice cubes

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a pitcher, combine cranberry juice, pomegranate juice, vanilla bean simple syrup, and freshly squeezed orange juice
  2. Step 2: whisk thoroughly until the syrup is fully blended and the mixture takes on a deep ruby color
  3. Step 3: add a generous handful of ice cubes to chill the base, then slowly pour club soda over the ice, allowing it to mingle without losing carbonation
  4. Step 4: sprinkle pomegranate seeds throughout the pitcher for visual sparkle and a burst of juicy texture
  5. Step 5: zest a fresh orange directly over the surface, letting the aromatic oils fall into the punch
  6. Step 6: give the mixture a gentle stir to distribute the zest and seeds evenly before serving
💡 Chef's Tip: Make the vanilla bean simple syrup ahead of time by simmering equal parts water and sugar with a split vanilla bean; store refrigerated for up to a week.

Serve in clear glasses with a few pomegranate seeds and a twist of orange zest; leftovers keep refrigerated for 24 hours, adding a splash more club soda before serving to restore the fizz.

10. Iced Pumpkin Spice Latte in Mason Jars

⏱️ 20 Mins (5m prep, 15m chill) 💰 Mid‑Range (~$3.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 4‑6
Save Pin
A chilled pumpkin spice latte layered over ice, topped with fluffy whipped cream and a dash of nutmeg.

This latte captures the quintessential fall flavors—creamy pumpkin puree, aromatic cinnamon, ginger, and clove—balanced by robust espresso. Served over ice, it delivers a refreshing chill while retaining the cozy warmth of traditional pumpkin spice. A dollop of lightly sweetened whipped cream adds silkiness, and a sprinkle of nutmeg finishes the experience with a fragrant kiss.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups strong brewed espresso or coffee
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 cup milk or oat milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ice cubes
  • whipped cream and ground nutmeg for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Brew espresso and let it cool slightly
  2. Step 2: in a shaker, whisk pumpkin puree, maple syrup, pumpkin pie spice, and milk until smooth
  3. Step 3: combine the espresso with the pumpkin mixture and shake vigorously with ice for 30 seconds
  4. Step 4: strain into mason jars filled with fresh ice
  5. Step 5: top each jar with a generous swirl of whipped cream and dust with ground nutmeg
  6. Step 6: insert a straw and serve immediately
💡 Chef's Tip: Use chilled pumpkin puree (store‑bought or pre‑made) to speed up the process and keep the latte extra cold.

Pair with cinnamon‑sugar biscotti for a sweet bite; leftovers can be stored in the fridge for up to 24 hours and shaken again before serving.

11. Cinnamon‑Infused Cider Punch in Elegant Glasses

⏱️ 25 Mins (10m prep, 15m simmer) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.20/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
A sparkling cider punch brightened with cinnamon and citrus, perfect for toasting the holiday.

This punch marries the crisp sweetness of apple cider with the warm, woody notes of cinnamon and a splash of fresh orange juice. The effervescent fizz from ginger ale adds lively bubbles, while a dash of bitters deepens the complexity. Aromatic and refreshing, it’s a crowd‑pleasing libation that balances sweet and spice beautifully.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 6 cups apple cider
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 cup fresh orange juice
  • 1 cup ginger ale
  • ½ cup dark rum (optional)
  • ¼ cup simple syrup
  • orange wheels and cinnamon sticks for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large pot, combine apple cider, cinnamon sticks, and orange juice

  3. Step 3: remove from heat, stir in simple syrup and rum if using, then let the mixture cool to room temperature
  4. Step 4: once cooled, pour into a punch bowl and add ginger ale for fizz
  5. Step 5: garnish with orange wheels and additional cinnamon sticks
  6. Step 6: serve in tall glasses over ice
💡 Chef's Tip: Make the spiced cider base a day ahead and chill; the flavors intensify, and you only need to add ginger ale right before serving.

Ideal served with a side of spiced nuts; store any leftover punch in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, discarding ginger ale after the first use.

16. I♥️ Cranberry Punch in a Classic Glass

⏱️ 18 Mins (8m prep, 10m chill) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$1.80/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6‑8
Save Pin
A bright cranberry punch brightened with lime and mint, delivering a refreshing holiday sip.

This punch blends the sharp, festive tartness of cranberry juice with the zesty brightness of lime and the cool freshness of mint. A hint of agave syrup softens the acidity, while sparkling water adds a light, bubbly texture. The result is a crisp, invigorating drink that’s both celebratory and easy to sip throughout the Thanksgiving day.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 4 cups cranberry juice
  • ½ cup fresh lime juice
  • ¼ cup agave syrup
  • 2 cups sparkling water
  • fresh mint leaves
  • lime wedges for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large pitcher, whisk together cranberry juice, lime juice, and agave syrup until fully combined
  2. Step 2: chill the mixture for 8‑10 minutes
  3. Step 3: just before serving, slowly stir in sparkling water to retain fizz
  4. Step 4: pour into glasses over ice, garnish each with a sprig of mint and a lime wedge
  5. Step 5: serve immediately
💡 Chef's Tip: Muddle a few mint leaves with the agave syrup before mixing for an extra burst of herbaceous flavor.

Pair with a light turkey sandwich or fresh veggie platter; any leftovers can be stored in the fridge for up to 2 days, adding a splash of sparkling water before the next round.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  4. Step 4: whisk in heavy cream until thickened
  5. Step 5: pour into mugs
  6. Step 6: top with whipped cream
  7. Step 7: sprinkle crushed candy cane
  8. Step 8: serve hot
💡 Chef's Tip: Use a double boiler or a heat‑proof bowl over simmering water to avoid scorching the chocolate.

Serve immediately with a spoon; keep the mugs warm by placing them in a preheated oven at 200°F for a few minutes before serving.

25. Sally's Slow‑Cooker Hot Chocolate with Marshmallows

⏱️ 2 hrs 15 min (15m prep, 2 hrs cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8
Save Pin
A velvety mug of slow‑cooked hot chocolate crowned with golden marshmallows, the aroma of cocoa and spices filling the kitchen.

This holiday classic marries the rich, deep notes of dark cocoa with the subtle warmth of cinnamon and a whisper of nutmeg. The slow‑cooker method infuses the chocolate with silky texture while the marshmallows soften into a cloud‑like topping. Each sip delivers a comforting sweetness balanced by a hint of spice, making it an instant crowd‑pleaser at Thanksgiving gatherings. The gentle simmer lets flavors mingle, creating a drink that feels both indulgent and cozy.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 12 oz bittersweet chocolate, chopped
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 3 cups mini marshmallows

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a medium saucepan, heat the milk, cream, brown sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg over medium heat until the sugar dissolves, about 5 minutes
  2. Step 2: do not boil
  3. Step 3: add the chopped chocolate and stir until fully melted and smooth
  4. Step 4: transfer the mixture to the slow‑cooker and set on low, letting it gently simmer for 90 minutes, stirring every 30 minutes
  5. Step 5: after 90 minutes, ladle the hot chocolate into mugs, topping each with a generous heap of mini marshmallows
  6. Step 6: allow the marshmallows to soften for 2–3 minutes before serving
  7. Step 7: serve immediately while the drink is steaming.
💡 Chef's Tip: For a richer flavor, replace half the milk with a splash of vanilla‑infused cream; if you prefer a dairy‑free version, swap the milk and cream for oat milk and coconut cream.

Pair this luscious hot chocolate with a side of freshly toasted pecans or a drizzle of caramel sauce; leftover drinks can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days, reheating gently on low heat.

Essential Party Food Prep & Hosting Secrets

  • Prep Cold Platters in Advance: Assemble charcuterie boards, dips, and desserts earlier in the day, cover with cling wrap, and chill so you can relax with guests.
  • Stagger Hot Oven Appetizers: Keep baking sheets with pre-assembled finger foods in the fridge and bake in waves so fresh, warm snacks flow continuously.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What foods should I prepare ahead of time?
A: Cold dips, truffle balls, snack mixes, and chocolate treats can easily be prepared 1-2 days in advance. Hot appetizers like wrapped sausages can be assembled ahead and baked fresh right as guests arrive.

Q: How much food should I plan per adult guest?
A: For a 2-3 hour cocktail party where a sit-down dinner is not served, plan on 6 to 8 appetizer bites per adult. If hosting during a traditional dinner hour, increase to 10 to 12 pieces per person.

Q: What foods can safely stay at room temperature?
A: Dry snacks and baked treats can stay out on the buffet table throughout the party. For perishable appetizers (cheeses, meats, and dips), adhere to the standard USDA 2-hour rule by serving in smaller batches replenished from the fridge.
