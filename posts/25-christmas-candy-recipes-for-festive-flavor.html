1. Aveline’s Bourbon‑Infused Holiday Balls

1. Aveline’s Bourbon‑Infused Holiday Balls

⏱️ 30 Mins (10m prep, 20m chill) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$1.80/serving) 🍽️ Serves 12‑15
Save Pin
Rich, boozy chocolate spheres that melt in your mouth and sparkle with holiday cheer.

These bite‑size delights blend deep dark chocolate with a whisper of bourbon, creating a warm, caramel‑kissed sweetness that’s perfect for winter gatherings. The outer dusting of cocoa adds a slightly bitter edge, balancing the buttery interior, while a hint of vanilla rounds out the aroma. Guests love the velvety texture that crumbles delicately with each bite, making these balls an instant crowd‑pleaser.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • ¼ cup bourbon
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • ¼ tsp sea salt
  • cocoa powder for rolling
  • festive red sprinkles (optional)

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Melt the dark chocolate in a heat‑proof bowl over a simmering pot of water, stirring until smooth, then remove from heat and let cool slightly
  2. Step 2: whisk softened butter, powdered sugar, sea salt, and bourbon together in a large bowl until light and fluffy, then fold in the melted chocolate until fully incorporated
  3. Step 3: cover the mixture and refrigerate for 15 minutes until firm enough to shape
  4. Step 4: using a teaspoon, scoop portions and roll between your palms to form smooth balls, then roll each ball in cocoa powder (and sprinkles if using) to coat evenly
  5. Step 5: place the finished balls on a parchment‑lined tray and chill for an additional 10 minutes to set before serving.
💡 Chef's Tip: Chill the mixing bowl and beaters for 10 minutes before whipping butter and sugar for a fluffier texture.

Serve these balls on a decorative platter with a side of vanilla bean whipped cream; store any leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.

2. Aveline’s Buckeye‑Style Peanut Butter Fudge

⏱️ 45 Mins (15m prep, 30m set) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.20/serving) 🍽️ Serves 10‑12
Save Pin
Decadent fudge swirled with peanut butter and dotted with chocolate “buckeyes” for a classic holiday treat.

This fudge marries silky chocolate with a rich peanut‑butter ribbon, delivering a sweet‑salty harmony that feels both nostalgic and luxe. The smooth, buttery base gives way to a nutty, slightly crunchy contrast from the chocolate‑dipped buckeye centers. Warm vanilla and a touch of sea salt heighten the flavor, while the glossy finish invites guests to dive right in.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups semi‑sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp sea salt
  • 1 cup confectioners’ sugar
  • 12 mini chocolate “buckeye” candies (optional)

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Line an 8‑inch square pan with parchment paper
  2. Step 2: in a microwave‑safe bowl, combine chocolate chips, butter, and peanut butter, heating in 30‑second intervals, stirring after each, until fully melted and glossy
  3. Step 3: stir in vanilla and sea salt, then whisk in confectioners’ sugar until the mixture is smooth and thick
  4. Step 4: pour the fudge into the prepared pan, smoothing the top with a spatula
  5. Step 5: if using, press mini chocolate “buckeye” candies into the surface, spacing them evenly
  6. Step 6: refrigerate for at least 30 minutes until firm, then cut into bite‑size squares.
💡 Chef's Tip: Use a silicone spatula to scrape every last bit of fudge from the bowl for a perfectly smooth top.

Present the fudge on a holiday-themed tray with a dusting of powdered sugar; keep leftovers refrigerated in an airtight container for up to a week.

3. Aveline’s Chocolate‑Peanut Butter Bliss Balls

⏱️ 25 Mins (10m prep, 15m chill) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$1.90/serving) 🍽️ Serves 14‑16
Save Pin
Silky chocolate‑peanut butter spheres rolled in crunchy peanuts for extra texture.

These indulgent balls fuse the deep richness of dark chocolate with the creamy, salty allure of peanut butter, creating a balanced sweet‑savory bite. A light coating of crushed peanuts adds a satisfying crunch that contrasts the melt‑in‑your‑mouth center. The aroma of toasted peanuts and cocoa fills the kitchen, making them irresistible as party finger foods.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • ¼ cup melted dark chocolate
  • ¼ tsp sea salt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • crushed roasted peanuts for coating

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a mixing bowl, combine peanut butter, powdered sugar, sea salt, and honey
  2. Step 2: beat with a hand mixer on medium speed until the mixture is smooth and glossy
  3. Step 3: drizzle in the melted dark chocolate and fold gently until fully incorporated, creating a marbled effect
  4. Step 4: cover the bowl and chill for 10 minutes to firm the mixture
  5. Step 5: using a small cookie scoop, portion out the mixture and roll each into a smooth ball between your palms
  6. Step 6: roll each ball in a shallow dish of crushed peanuts, pressing gently to adhere
  7. Step 7: place the finished balls on parchment paper and chill for an additional 5 minutes before serving.
💡 Chef's Tip: Freeze the peanut butter mixture for 20 minutes if it becomes too soft while shaping.

Serve these balls alongside a small dish of chocolate ganache for dipping; store in a sealed container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.

4. Aveline’s Layered Chocolate‑Peanut Butter Stacks

⏱️ 35 Mins (15m prep, 20m set) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 10‑12
Save Pin
Crunchy peanut butter cores sandwiched between crisp chocolate layers for a textural masterpiece.

These elegant stacks deliver a symphony of textures—crisp chocolate shards, creamy peanut butter filling, and a final glossy chocolate glaze. The sweet‑salty balance is highlighted by a whisper of sea salt, while the aroma of toasted peanuts and rich cocoa creates a festive sensory experience. Perfect as party finger foods, each bite offers a satisfying snap followed by a melt‑in‑the‑mouth finish.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup dark chocolate chips
  • ½ cup creamy peanut butter
  • ¼ cup confectioners’ sugar
  • ¼ tsp sea salt
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • cocoa powder for dusting

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Line a baking sheet with parchment and set a silicone mold (or use a mini muffin tin) upside down
  2. Step 2: melt dark chocolate chips in a microwave in 30‑second bursts, stirring until smooth
  3. Step 3: spoon a thin layer of melted chocolate into each mold cavity, spreading to the edges, then refrigerate for 5 minutes to set
  4. Step 4: meanwhile, blend peanut butter, confectioners’ sugar, sea salt, and melted butter until smooth
  5. Step 5: once the first chocolate layer is firm, pipe a small dollop of the peanut butter mixture onto each, then top with another thin chocolate layer, smoothing the surface
  6. Step 6: refrigerate the assembled stacks for 15 minutes until fully set
  7. Step 7: gently pop them out and dust lightly with cocoa powder.
💡 Chef's Tip: Use a small offset spatula to spread chocolate evenly for a uniform thickness.

Arrange the stacks on a decorative platter with fresh cranberries for color contrast; store in a cool, dry place in an airtight container for up to 3 days.

5. Aveline’s Chocolate‑Peanut Butter Swirl Fudge

⏱️ 40 Mins (15m prep, 25m chill) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.10/serving) 🍽️ Serves 12‑14
Save Pin
Silky chocolate fudge interlaced with creamy peanut butter ribbons for a festive sweet‑salty swirl.

This fudge offers a luxurious blend of rich, velvety chocolate and a smooth, nutty peanut butter ribbon that creates a striking marbled appearance. The sweet‑salty contrast is amplified by a pinch of sea salt, while the aroma of roasted peanuts and cocoa fills the room, making it a standout festive dessert. Its dense, melt‑in‑your‑mouth texture keeps guests reaching for more.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups milk chocolate chips
  • ½ cup creamy peanut butter
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp sea salt
  • 1 cup confectioners’ sugar
  • optional chopped peanuts for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Line an 8‑inch square pan with parchment paper
  2. Step 2: melt milk chocolate chips and butter together in a microwave‑safe bowl, heating in 30‑second intervals and stirring until smooth
  3. Step 3: stir in vanilla and sea salt
  4. Step 4: in a separate bowl, whisk together confectioners’ sugar and peanut butter until creamy
  5. Step 5: pour the chocolate mixture into the prepared pan, spreading evenly
  6. Step 6: drop spoonfuls of the peanut butter mixture over the chocolate, then use a butter knife to swirl gently, creating ribbons
  7. Step 7: sprinkle chopped peanuts on top if desired
  8. Step 8: refrigerate for at least 25 minutes until firm, then cut into squares.
💡 Chef's Tip: For a glossy finish, gently tap the pan on the counter after swirling to settle the layers.

Serve chilled on a holiday platter with fresh berries for a pop of color; keep leftovers refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 5 days.

2. Aveline’s Foolproof Holiday Fudge

6. Aveline’s Foolproof Holiday Fudge

⏱️ 35 Mins (10m prep, 25m set) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$1.75/serving) 🍽️ Serves 16‑18
Save Pin
Smooth, melt‑in‑your‑mouth fudge that stays perfectly creamy every time.

This foolproof fudge delivers a consistently creamy texture with a deep chocolate flavor that’s both rich and comforting. A subtle hint of vanilla and a pinch of sea salt elevate the sweetness without overpowering, while the faint aroma of cocoa makes it instantly inviting. Its simplicity makes it an ideal base for adding festive toppings or serving as a standalone holiday dessert.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups semi‑sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp sea salt
  • powdered sugar for dusting
  • optional crushed peppermint for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Line a 9‑inch square pan with parchment paper
  2. Step 2: combine chocolate chips, sweetened condensed milk, and butter in a microwave‑safe bowl
  3. Step 3: heat in 45‑second intervals, stirring after each, until the mixture is completely melted and smooth
  4. Step 4: remove from heat and stir in vanilla and sea salt
  5. Step 5: pour the mixture into the prepared pan, spreading evenly with a spatula
  6. Step 6: if desired, sprinkle crushed peppermint over the top and gently press in
  7. Step 7: refrigerate for 25 minutes until firm
  8. Step 8: lift the fudge out using the parchment overhang and cut into squares.
💡 Chef's Tip: Use a candy thermometer and stop heating when the mixture reaches 115°F (46°C) for perfect set‑up.

Dust each square lightly with powdered sugar before serving for an elegant finish; store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 7 days.

7. Aveline’s Graham Cracker Toffee Crunch

⏱️ 45 Mins (15m prep, 30m bake) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.30/serving) 🍽️ Serves 12‑14
Save Pin
Sweet, buttery toffee layered with crunchy graham crumbs and a glossy chocolate drizzle.

This toffee combines buttery caramel richness with the nostalgic crunch of graham crackers, creating a sweet‑salty symphony that’s both buttery and airy. The aroma of toasted graham and caramelized sugar fills the kitchen, while a final drizzle of dark chocolate adds depth and a glossy finish. Its crisp texture makes it an ideal festive dessert or party finger food.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp sea salt
  • 2 cups crushed graham crackers
  • ½ cup semi‑sweet chocolate chips
  • extra crushed graham for topping

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment
  2. Step 2: melt butter and brown sugar together in a heavy saucepan over medium heat, stirring constantly until the mixture bubbles and turns a deep amber color (about 5‑7 minutes)
  3. Step 3: remove from heat and stir in vanilla and sea salt
  4. Step 4: spread the hot toffee evenly onto the prepared sheet, then sprinkle crushed graham crackers over the surface, pressing gently to embed
  5. Step 5: bake for 12‑15 minutes until the edges turn a light golden brown
  6. Step 6: remove from oven and immediately drizzle melted chocolate chips over the hot toffee
  7. Step 7: allow to cool completely on a wire rack, then break into rustic bars.
💡 Chef's Tip: For extra crunch, toast the graham crackers lightly before crushing them.

Serve the bars with a side of whipped cream or vanilla ice cream for an indulgent treat; store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.

8. Aveline’s Loaded Christmas Peppermint Bark

⏱️ 30 Mins (10m prep, 20m set) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 15‑20
Save Pin
Colorful peppermint bark packed with candy cane shards, dried cranberries, and toasted almonds for a dazzling holiday treat.

This loaded bark offers a delightful contrast between silky dark chocolate and sweet white chocolate, punctuated by crisp peppermint shards, tart cranberries, and buttery toasted almonds. The aromatic peppermint and rich cocoa create a festive aroma that instantly transports you to a winter wonderland. Its crunchy, chewy, and melt‑in‑your‑mouth textures make it a show‑stopping addition to any holiday spread.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 8 oz white chocolate, chopped
  • ½ cup crushed peppermint candy canes
  • ¼ cup dried cranberries
  • ¼ cup toasted slivered almonds
  • sea salt flakes for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper
  2. Step 2: melt dark chocolate in a microwave‑safe bowl in 30‑second bursts, stirring until smooth
  3. Step 3: spread the melted dark chocolate evenly on the parchment, creating a thin layer
  4. Step 4: place the sheet in the refrigerator for 5 minutes to set slightly
  5. Step 5: meanwhile, melt white chocolate using the same method
  6. Step 6: drizzle the white chocolate over the semi‑set dark layer, using a fork to create a marbled effect
  7. Step 7: immediately sprinkle crushed peppermint, dried cranberries, and toasted almonds over the top, then lightly press to adhere
  8. Step 8: finish with a pinch of sea salt flakes
  9. Step 9: return the bark to the refrigerator for 15 minutes until fully hardened
  10. Step 10: break into irregular shards and arrange on a festive serving board.
💡 Chef's Tip: Use a kitchen torch to lightly melt the surface of the dark chocolate before adding white chocolate for a seamless swirl.

Pair the bark with a hot mug of spiced cocoa or mulled wine for an extra festive touch; store in a cool, dry place in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks.

9. Averie's Peanut Butter Butterscotch Fudge

⏱️ 35 Mins (10m prep, 25m set) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 12-15
Save Pin
A silky, caramel‑sweet fudge studded with crunchy peanut butter swirls, perfect for Christmas gatherings.

This fudge marries the nutty depth of creamy peanut butter with buttery, buttery‑rich butterscotch. Each bite delivers a smooth, melt‑in‑your‑mouth texture that snaps into a satisfying chew as it cools. Warm vanilla and a hint of sea salt elevate the sweet profile, while a faint caramel aroma fills the kitchen. Guests love its nostalgic holiday flavor with a modern twist.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups dark brown sugar
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup creamy peanut butter
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch of sea salt
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine brown sugar, heavy cream, butter, and golden syrup
  2. Step 2: whisk until sugar dissolves and mixture comes to a gentle boil
  3. Step 3: continue boiling for 4‑5 minutes until a soft‑ball stage (115‑118°C) is reached
  4. Step 4: remove from heat and stir in peanut butter, vanilla, and sea salt until fully incorporated and glossy
  5. Step 5: pour into a parchment‑lined 8×8‑inch pan, smooth the top, and let set at room temperature for 20‑25 minutes before cutting into squares
💡 Chef's Tip: For ultra‑smooth fudge, strain the hot mixture through a fine‑mesh sieve before adding the peanut butter.

Serve chilled on a decorative platter with a dusting of crushed peanuts; store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week.

10. Averie's Special K Holiday Bars

⏱️ 45 Mins (15m prep, 30m bake) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.75/serving) 🍽️ Serves 10-12
Save Pin
Crunchy Special K bars layered with caramel and white chocolate, speckled with dried cranberries for holiday cheer.

These bars combine the light, airy crunch of Special K cereal with a buttery caramel base, creating a sweet‑nutty foundation. A silky white chocolate glaze adds a festive sparkle, while tart dried cranberries provide a bright contrast. The aroma of toasted oats and caramel fills the air, making them an instant crowd‑pleaser at any holiday spread. Their portable size makes them perfect for gift bags or dessert tables.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 3 cups Special K cereal
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 1 cup white chocolate chips
  • ¼ cup dried cranberries
  • pinch of cinnamon

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 9×13‑inch pan with parchment
  2. Step 2: melt butter, brown sugar, and honey in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until smooth
  3. Step 3: pour the caramel over Special K and oats, toss to coat evenly
  4. Step 4: spread mixture into the pan and bake for 12‑15 minutes until lightly golden
  5. Step 5: meanwhile, melt white chocolate chips in a microwave or double boiler
  6. Step 6: drizzle melted white chocolate over the warm bars, then sprinkle dried cranberries
  7. Step 7: let cool completely before slicing into bars
💡 Chef's Tip: Press the mixture firmly with a flat spatula or the bottom of a measuring cup to ensure the bars hold together after cooling.

Serve at room temperature with a side of hot cocoa; store in an airtight container for up to 5 days or freeze for longer keeping.

13. Charlatan's Classic English Toffee

⏱️ 30 Mins (10m prep, 20m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.80/serving) 🍽️ Serves 20-24 pieces
Save Pin
Brittle, buttery toffee studded with toasted almonds, baked to a perfect amber hue.

This English toffee boasts a deep, buttery caramel base that crackles delightfully under the bite. Toasted almonds lend a nutty crunch and a subtle earthy note, while a pinch of sea salt lifts the sweetness to sophisticated heights. The intoxicating scent of caramelized butter fills the kitchen, making it an irresistible holiday staple that guests adore.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup golden syrup
  • 1 cup all‑purpose flour
  • 1 cup sliced almonds, toasted
  • pinch of sea salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment
  2. Step 2: melt butter, brown sugar, and golden syrup together in a heavy saucepan over medium heat, stirring until bubbling and smooth
  3. Step 3: continue cooking for 5‑6 minutes until the mixture reaches 300°F (hard‑ball stage) on a candy thermometer
  4. Step 4: remove from heat, stir in flour, vanilla, and sea salt until just combined
  5. Step 5: spread the toffee onto the prepared sheet, smoothing to an even layer
  6. Step 6: sprinkle toasted almonds across the surface
  7. Step 7: bake for 12‑15 minutes until edges turn a deep amber
  8. Step 8: cool completely before breaking into pieces
💡 Chef's Tip: Use a silicone spatula to spread the hot toffee quickly—its thickness sets fast, and a thin layer yields crispier bites.

Pair with a glass of chilled port or a rich black tea; store in an airtight tin for up to a month.

14. Charlatan's Crunchy Peanut Brittle

⏱️ 35 Mins (10m prep, 25m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.60/serving) 🍽️ Serves 30-35 pieces
Save Pin
Thin, caramelized peanut brittle with a glossy finish and a satisfying snap.

This brittle delivers a buttery caramel base that hardens into a delicate, glass‑like shell. Roasted peanuts provide a hearty crunch and a nutty aroma that balances the sweetness. A whisper of vanilla and a pinch of sea salt enhance depth, while the caramel’s rich scent invites you to snap a piece and savor. Its bright amber color and crackling texture make it a show‑stopping candy for any Christmas gathering.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup light corn syrup
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 cup roasted peanuts, unsalted
  • ¼ cup butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch of sea salt

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Line a large baking sheet with parchment
  2. Step 2: in a heavy saucepan combine sugar, corn syrup, and water
  3. Step 3: cook over medium‑high heat, stirring until sugar dissolves, then stop stirring and let the mixture boil until it reaches 300°F (hard‑crack stage) on a candy thermometer
  4. Step 4: quickly stir in butter, peanuts, vanilla, and sea salt, mixing until evenly coated
  5. Step 5: immediately pour the hot mixture onto the prepared sheet, spreading with a spatula to a thin layer
  6. Step 6: allow to cool completely at room temperature, then break into shards
💡 Chef's Tip: Work in a well‑ventilated kitchen and keep a bowl of ice water nearby for any accidental burns.

Serve alongside mulled wine for a warm contrast; store brittle in a sealed container with parchment layers to prevent sticking.

16. Charlatan's Caramelized Pecan Pralines

⏱️ 45 Mins (15m prep, 30m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 20-24 pralines

Sweet, buttery caramel pralines studded with crunchy toasted pecans and a light dusting of sea salt.

Essential Party Food Prep & Hosting Secrets

  • Prep Cold Platters in Advance: Assemble charcuterie boards, dips, and desserts earlier in the day, cover with cling wrap, and chill so you can relax with guests.
  • Stagger Hot Oven Appetizers: Keep baking sheets with pre-assembled finger foods in the fridge and bake in waves so fresh, warm snacks flow continuously.
  • Smart Grocery Budget Swaps: Purchasing block cheese to shred or cube at home delivers superior melting and noticeably trims your grocery bill compared to pre-shredded bags.
  • Strategic Grazing Stations: Separate savory finger foods, dips, and desserts onto different tables to prevent kitchen bottlenecks.
  • Effortless Edible Garnishes: Fresh herbs, colorful drizzles, and festive toppings turn simple recipes into viral Pinterest-worthy centerpieces!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What foods should I prepare ahead of time?
A: Cold dips, truffle balls, snack mixes, and chocolate treats can easily be prepared 1-2 days in advance. Hot appetizers like wrapped sausages can be assembled ahead and baked fresh right as guests arrive.

Q: How much food should I plan per adult guest?
A: For a 2-3 hour cocktail party where a sit-down dinner is not served, plan on 6 to 8 appetizer bites per adult. If hosting during a traditional dinner hour, increase to 10 to 12 pieces per person.

Q: What foods can safely stay at room temperature?
A: Dry snacks and baked treats can stay out on the buffet table throughout the party. For perishable appetizers (cheeses, meats, and dips), adhere to the standard USDA 2-hour rule by serving in smaller batches replenished from the fridge.
