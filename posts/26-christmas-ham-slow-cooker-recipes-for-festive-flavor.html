1. Maple‑Brown Sugar Glazed Ham

1. Maple‑Brown Sugar Glazed Ham

⏱️ 4 Hours (15m prep, 3h45m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
A sweet‑savory glaze transforms this ham into a glossy, aromatic centerpiece that beckons guests to the table.

This ham sings with the warm richness of pure maple syrup balanced by the deep caramel notes of brown sugar, while a hint of Dijon adds subtle tang. The slow cooker keeps the meat buttery tender, and the glaze forms a sticky, caramelized crust that crackles with every slice. Aromas of toasted rosemary and cracked black pepper fill the kitchen, making it an instant crowd‑pleaser at any holiday feast.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (8‑lb) bone‑in smoked ham
  • 1 cup pure maple syrup
  • ½ cup dark brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary, leaves stripped

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Pat the ham dry with paper towels and score the surface in a diamond pattern about ¼‑inch deep, creating shallow cuts for the glaze to seep in
  2. Step 2: Place the ham fat side up in the slow cooker, ensuring it fits snugly without folding
  3. Step 3: In a saucepan, combine maple syrup, brown sugar, Dijon, and black pepper
  4. Step 4: bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring until sugar dissolves and the mixture thickens slightly, about 3‑4 minutes
  5. Step 5: Pour half of the glaze over the ham, reserving the rest for basting
  6. Step 6: Cover and cook on low for 3 hours, then switch to high for the final 45 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 15 minutes
  7. Step 7: When the glaze is thick and glossy and the internal temperature reaches 140°F, remove the ham, let rest 10 minutes, then slice and garnish with rosemary leaves.
💡 Chef's Tip: For an extra glossy finish, brush the ham with a thin layer of melted butter mixed with a splash of the glaze just before the final high‑heat stage.

Serve warm with a side of grainy mustard or a dollop of apple chutney; leftover slices reheat beautifully wrapped in foil and warmed gently in a 300°F oven for 15 minutes.

2. Pineapple‑Honey Bourbon Ham

⏱️ 5 Hours (20m prep, 4h40m cook) 💰 Moderate (~$3.75/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
A tropical twist of pineapple and bourbon infuses this ham with sweet heat and festive flair.

The bright acidity of crushed pineapple balances the deep, caramel notes of honey and bourbon, creating a sweet‑spicy glaze that clings to every slice. Slow cooking melds the flavors, yielding a tender, juicy ham with a slightly smoky undertone. A final garnish of toasted pecans adds crunch, while the aroma of caramelized fruit fills the room, making it a memorable holiday main.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (7‑lb) smoked ham
  • 1 cup crushed pineapple, drained
  • ½ cup honey
  • ¼ cup bourbon
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • ¼ cup toasted pecans, chopped

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Slice shallow cuts into the ham’s surface in a crosshatch pattern, about ¼‑inch deep
  2. Step 2: Arrange the ham in the slow cooker, fat side up, and set aside
  3. Step 3: In a small saucepan, combine crushed pineapple, honey, bourbon, and apple cider vinegar
  4. Step 4: bring to a gentle boil over medium‑high heat, then reduce to a simmer, stirring constantly until the sauce thickens to a syrupy consistency, about 6‑8 minutes
  5. Step 5: Pour half of the pineapple‑bourbon glaze over the ham, reserving the remainder for later basting
  6. Step 6: Cover and cook on low for 4 hours, then increase to high for 40 minutes, basting with the saved glaze every 10 minutes
  7. Step 7: During the last 10 minutes, sprinkle toasted pecans over the top, allowing them to toast lightly in the steam
  8. Step 8: Verify the ham reaches 140°F, then remove, rest 10 minutes, slice, and drizzle with any leftover glaze.
💡 Chef's Tip: Freeze the pineapple‑bourbon glaze in ice‑cube trays; you’ll have ready‑to‑use portions for future meals or quick glaze upgrades.

Pair with buttery corn pudding or roasted Brussels sprouts for a balanced plate; leftovers keep up to 4 days refrigerated, reheated gently in a skillet with a splash of pineapple juice.

3. Mustard‑Maple Dijon Ham

⏱️ 4 Hours (15m prep, 3h45m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.80/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6‑8
Save Pin
A tangy‑sweet mustard glaze gives this ham a bright, glossy coat that’s perfect for holiday spreads.

The sharp bite of Dijon mingles with the natural sweetness of maple syrup, while whole grain mustard seeds add texture and a pop of flavor. Slow cooking ensures the ham stays moist, and the glaze caramelizes into a glossy, slightly sticky crust that’s both visually striking and delicious. A whisper of smoked paprika adds a subtle smoky depth, making this dish a standout among festive mains.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (6‑lb) bone‑in ham
  • ¾ cup pure maple syrup
  • ¼ cup Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp whole grain mustard
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cloves

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Trim excess fat from the ham, then score the surface in a diagonal diamond pattern about ¼‑inch deep
  2. Step 2: Place the ham in the slow cooker, fat side up, ensuring it sits flat
  3. Step 3: In a bowl, whisk together maple syrup, Dijon mustard, whole grain mustard, smoked paprika, and ground cloves until smooth
  4. Step 4: Pour half of the mixture over the ham, reserving the rest for basting
  5. Step 5: Cover and cook on low for 3 hours, then switch to high for the final 45 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 15 minutes
  6. Step 6: When the glaze is thick, caramelized, and the internal temperature hits 140°F, remove the ham, let rest 10 minutes, then slice and serve.
💡 Chef's Tip: Add a splash of apple cider to the glaze for a brighter acidity that cuts through the richness.

Serve alongside creamy mashed sweet potatoes or a crisp green salad with citrus vinaigrette; leftovers reheat well in a covered dish with a drizzle of maple‑mustard glaze to prevent drying out.

4. Orange‑Ginger Glazed Ham

⏱️ 4½ Hours (20m prep, 4h30m cook) 💰 Moderate (~$3.20/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
Zesty orange and warm ginger create a fragrant glaze that lifts this ham into a bright, holiday‑ready masterpiece.

Fresh orange zest and juice deliver a citrusy sparkle, while grated ginger adds a gentle heat that awakens the palate. The glaze thickens into a sticky, amber coating that caramelizes beautifully in the slow cooker, infusing the ham with a fragrant, slightly spicy aroma. This combination of sweet, tangy, and warm notes makes the dish a vibrant centerpiece that dazzles both eyes and taste buds.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (7‑lb) smoked ham
  • 1 cup fresh orange juice
  • zest of 1 orange
  • 2 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Score the ham’s surface in a crosshatch pattern about ¼‑inch deep
  2. Step 2: Place the ham in the slow cooker, fat side up
  3. Step 3: In a saucepan, combine orange juice, orange zest, grated ginger, brown sugar, soy sauce, and cinnamon
  4. Step 4: bring to a boil over medium heat, then reduce to a simmer, stirring until the sugar dissolves and the mixture reduces by one‑third, about 8‑10 minutes
  5. Step 5: Pour half of the orange‑ginger glaze over the ham, reserving the rest for basting
  6. Step 6: Cover and cook on low for 4 hours, then increase to high for the final 30 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 10 minutes
  7. Step 7: Ensure the ham reaches 140°F, then remove, let rest 10 minutes, slice, and drizzle with any leftover glaze.
💡 Chef's Tip: For extra sparkle, add a tablespoon of finely chopped candied orange peel to the glaze just before serving.

Pair with roasted root vegetables or a light quinoa salad with cranberries; leftovers store in airtight containers and reheat gently with a splash of orange juice to revive the glaze.

5. Bourbon‑Brown Sugar Chipotle Ham

⏱️ 5 Hours (25m prep, 4h35m cook) 💰 Moderate (~$3.60/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
A bold chipotle‑infused bourbon glaze gives this ham a smoky, sweet heat that’s perfect for daring holiday palates.

Rich bourbon and deep brown sugar create a luscious sweet base, while chipotle powder adds a smoky, lingering heat that balances the sweetness. The slow cooker melds these flavors, producing a tender ham with a dark, caramelized crust that releases a tantalizing aroma of smoked spice. This daring flavor profile is a hit for guests who love a touch of heat with their holiday comfort.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (8‑lb) bone‑in ham
  • ½ cup bourbon
  • ¾ cup dark brown sugar
  • 1 tsp chipotle powder
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • ¼ tsp ground allspice

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Pat the ham dry and score the surface in a shallow diamond pattern
  2. Step 2: Place the ham in the slow cooker, fat side up
  3. Step 3: In a small bowl, whisk together bourbon, brown sugar, chipotle powder, Dijon mustard, and allspice until smooth
  4. Step 4: Pour half of the mixture over the ham, reserving the rest for basting
  5. Step 5: Cover and cook on low for 4 hours, then switch to high for 35 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 10 minutes
  6. Step 6: When the glaze turns dark mahogany and the internal temperature reaches 140°F, remove the ham, let rest 10 minutes, then slice and garnish with chopped cilantro.
💡 Chef's Tip: Soak a handful of chipotle peppers in adobo sauce and blend into the glaze for an intensified smoky flavor.

Serve alongside creamy coleslaw or a buttery corn casserole; leftovers reheat in a skillet with a splash of bourbon to revive the glaze’s richness.

2. Cranberry‑Apple Wine Ham

6. Cranberry‑Apple Wine Ham

⏱️ 4 Hours (20m prep, 3h40m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.90/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
Tart cranberries and sweet apples mingle with red wine for a glossy, festive glaze that brightens the holiday table.

The tartness of fresh cranberries pairs beautifully with the natural sweetness of apples, while red wine adds depth and a subtle acidity. As the glaze reduces, it becomes a glossy, ruby‑red coating that caramelizes on the ham, delivering a harmonious balance of sweet, sour, and savory notes. Aromas of rosemary and simmered fruit fill the kitchen, making this dish a fragrant centerpiece for any celebration.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (7‑lb) smoked ham
  • 1 cup fresh cranberries
  • 1 cup apple cider
  • ½ cup dry red wine
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 1 tsp fresh rosemary, minced
  • pinch of salt

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Score the ham in a crosshatch pattern
  2. Step 2: Place the ham in the slow cooker, fat side up
  3. Step 3: In a saucepan, combine cranberries, apple cider, red wine, honey, rosemary, and salt
  4. Step 4: bring to a boil over medium‑high heat, then reduce to a simmer, stirring occasionally until the cranberries burst and the sauce reduces to a thick glaze, about 12‑15 minutes
  5. Step 5: Strain the glaze through a fine mesh to remove skins, then pour half over the ham, reserving the rest for basting
  6. Step 6: Cover and cook on low for 3 hours, then switch to high for the final 40 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 10 minutes
  7. Step 7: Once the ham reaches 140°F and the glaze is glossy, remove, rest 10 minutes, slice, and drizzle with any leftover sauce.
💡 Chef's Tip: Freeze extra cranberry‑apple glaze in silicone molds for future use on pork chops or roasted vegetables.

Pair with roasted sweet potatoes or a wild rice pilaf studded with dried cranberries; leftovers store in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat gently with a splash of apple cider to keep the glaze moist.

7. Honey‑Sriracha Glazed Ham

⏱️ 4½ Hours (15m prep, 4h30m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.10/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
Sweet honey meets spicy sriracha for a bold, glossy glaze that adds a modern kick to classic holiday ham.

The natural sweetness of honey balances the fiery heat of sriracha, creating a glaze that’s both sticky and vibrant. As the ham slow‑cooks, the glaze caramelizes into a glossy, slightly smoky crust that offers a delightful contrast of sweet and spicy with each bite. A finish of toasted sesame seeds adds a subtle nutty crunch, while the aroma of caramelized chili awakens the senses, making this dish a conversation starter at any holiday table.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (6‑lb) smoked ham
  • ½ cup honey
  • ¼ cup sriracha sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • pinch of crushed red pepper flakes

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Score the ham lightly in a diamond pattern
  2. Step 2: Place the ham in the slow cooker, fat side up
  3. Step 3: In a bowl, whisk together honey, sriracha, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and red pepper flakes until smooth
  4. Step 4: Pour half of the mixture over the ham, reserving the rest for basting
  5. Step 5: Cover and cook on low for 4 hours, then increase to high for the final 30 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 10 minutes
  6. Step 6: During the last 5 minutes, sprinkle toasted sesame seeds over the top to toast lightly
  7. Step 7: Verify the ham reaches an internal temperature of 140°F and the glaze is thick and glossy, then remove, rest 10 minutes, slice, and serve with any leftover glaze.
💡 Chef's Tip: Mix a teaspoon of cornstarch with cold water and stir into the glaze during the final basting to achieve a thicker, clingier coating.

Serve alongside steamed broccoli tossed in garlic butter or a cool cucumber salad to balance the heat; leftovers reheat in a microwave‑safe dish covered with a damp paper towel to retain moisture.

8. Bourbon‑Apple Cider Glazed Ham

⏱️ 5 Hours (20m prep, 4h40m cook) 💰 Moderate (~$3.45/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
A buttery bourbon‑apple cider glaze creates a caramel‑kissed ham that’s both sweet and aromatic.

The sweet, crisp flavor of apple cider pairs with the warm, oaky notes of bourbon, forming a glaze that’s both buttery and slightly tangy. Slow cooking allows the ham to absorb these flavors, while the glaze reduces to a caramel‑rich coating that glistens with a deep amber hue. Aromas of baked apples, toasted thyme, and a hint of vanilla fill the kitchen, delivering a comforting, festive taste that feels like holiday magic in every bite.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (7‑lb) smoked ham
  • 1 cup apple cider
  • ¼ cup bourbon
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme, leaves stripped
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Score the ham’s surface in shallow diamonds
  2. Step 2: Place the ham in the slow cooker, fat side up
  3. Step 3: In a saucepan, combine apple cider, bourbon, brown sugar, vanilla, and thyme leaves
  4. Step 4: bring to a boil over medium‑high heat, then reduce to a simmer, stirring until the mixture thickens to a syrupy consistency, about 10‑12 minutes
  5. Step 5: Remove thyme sprigs, then whisk in butter until melted and glossy
  6. Step 6: Pour half of the bourbon‑apple glaze over the ham, reserving the rest for basting
  7. Step 7: Cover and cook on low for 4 hours, then switch to high for the final 40 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 10 minutes
  8. Step 8: When the ham reaches 140°F and the glaze is caramelized, remove, let rest 10 minutes, slice, and garnish with thin apple slices and a sprinkle of fresh thyme.
💡 Chef's Tip: Use a splash of Calvados (apple brandy) instead of bourbon for an even more pronounced apple flavor.

Serve with roasted Brussels sprouts tossed in bacon or a creamy parsnip mash; leftovers store in the fridge for up to 4 days and reheat gently in a covered dish with a drizzle of apple cider to keep the glaze luscious.

Save Pin
A sweet‑savory maple‑brown sugar glaze coats every slice with glossy caramelized goodness.

This ham bursts with a buttery sweetness from pure maple syrup balanced by the deep caramel notes of brown sugar. The glaze creates a sticky, slightly crisp crust while the interior stays tender and juicy. Warm aromas of toasted nuts and a hint of rosemary drift from the slow cooker, making it an instant crowd‑pleaser at any holiday table.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (8‑lb) bone‑in smoked ham
  • 1/2 cup pure maple syrup
  • 1/3 cup dark brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • fresh rosemary sprigs for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Trim excess rind from the ham, leaving a thin layer of fat
  2. Step 2: place ham fat side up in the slow cooker

  4. Step 4: pour half the glaze over the ham, reserving the rest for basting
  5. Step 5: cook on low for 2½‑3 hrs, basting with reserved glaze every 45 minutes
  6. Step 6: when the internal temperature hits 140°F, switch to high for 15 minutes to caramelize the crust, then let rest 10 minutes before slicing
💡 Chef's Tip: For an extra glossy finish, brush the ham with a thin layer of melted butter mixed with a splash of maple syrup just before serving.

10. Citrus‑Honey Mustard Ham

⏱️ 2 hrs 45 mins (20m prep, 2h25m cook) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.80/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
Zesty citrus meets sweet honey in a mustard‑kissed glaze that brightens every bite.

The tangy zest of orange and lemon lifts the savory ham, while honey adds a mellow sweetness. A smooth mustard base provides a subtle heat that rounds out the flavor profile. Aromas of citrus peel and caramelized honey fill the kitchen, promising a lively palate experience.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (7‑lb) fully cooked ham
  • 1/4 cup orange zest
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 3 tbsp whole‑grain mustard
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Pat the ham dry and score a diamond pattern into the surface
  2. Step 2: in a bowl whisk orange zest, lemon juice, honey, mustard, smoked paprika, and olive oil until smooth
  3. Step 3: pour half the mixture over the ham, reserving the rest for later
  4. Step 4: set the slow cooker to low and cook the ham for 2 hrs, then increase to high for the final 25 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 10 minutes
  5. Step 5: check that the interior reaches 140°F before removing
  6. Step 6: let rest 10 minutes before slicing
💡 Chef's Tip: Add a handful of sliced orange segments to the slow cooker during the last 30 minutes for extra burst of citrus aroma.

Pair with a crisp mixed green salad tossed in a citrus vinaigrette; store leftovers in airtight containers for up to 4 days, reheating gently with a drizzle of honey.

11. Spiced Pineapple Rum Ham

⏱️ 3 hrs (25m prep, 2h35m cook) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$3.20/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8‑10
Save Pin
A tropical rum‑infused glaze with pineapple and warm spices turns this ham into a vacation‑in‑a‑bite.

Sweet pineapple and dark rum mingle with aromatic spices like cloves and allspice, delivering a rich, island‑style flavor. The glaze caramelizes into a glossy, slightly sticky coating while the ham stays moist and tender. Warm, fragrant notes of rum and toasted sugar create an irresistible holiday centerpiece.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (8‑lb) bone‑in ham
  • 1 cup pineapple juice
  • 1/4 cup dark rum
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cloves
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • pineapple rings for garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Score the ham’s surface in a crosshatch pattern
  2. Step 2: melt butter in a saucepan, then stir in pineapple juice, rum, brown sugar, cloves, and allspice
  3. Step 3: bring to a gentle boil, reduce to a thick syrup (about 5 minutes)
  4. Step 4: pour half the glaze over the ham in the slow cooker, reserving the rest
  5. Step 5: cook on low for 2 hrs, then switch to high for the final 35 minutes, basting with remaining glaze every 10 minutes
  6. Step 6: ensure internal temperature reaches 140°F before removing
  7. Step 7: rest 10 minutes, then garnish with pineapple rings
💡 Chef's Tip: For deeper rum flavor, soak the ham in a rum‑pineapple brine overnight before cooking.

Serve alongside coconut‑infused rice and a fresh mango salsa; leftovers freeze well wrapped tightly for up to 2 months, thawing in the refrigerator before reheating.

14. Herb‑Garlic Butter Ham

Save Pin
A silky herb‑garlic butter bath infuses each slice with aromatic richness and a buttery sheen.

Fresh herbs and garlic melt into a buttery glaze that seeps into every fiber of the ham, delivering a luscious, melt‑in‑your‑mouth experience. The subtle peppery notes of thyme and rosemary balance the richness, while a faint caramelization adds a gentle crunch. The aroma of garlic and herbs wafts invitingly, making it a comforting holiday staple.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 (8‑lb) bone‑in ham
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  6. Step 6: ensure internal temperature reaches 140°F

15. Chipotle‑Brown Sugar Ham

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What foods should I prepare ahead of time?
A: Cold dips, truffle balls, snack mixes, and chocolate treats can easily be prepared 1-2 days in advance. Hot appetizers like wrapped sausages can be assembled ahead and baked fresh right as guests arrive.

Q: How much food should I plan per adult guest?
A: For a 2-3 hour cocktail party where a sit-down dinner is not served, plan on 6 to 8 appetizer bites per adult. If hosting during a traditional dinner hour, increase to 10 to 12 pieces per person.

Q: What foods can safely stay at room temperature?
A: Dry snacks and baked treats can stay out on the buffet table throughout the party. For perishable appetizers (cheeses, meats, and dips), adhere to the standard USDA 2-hour rule by serving in smaller batches replenished from the fridge.
